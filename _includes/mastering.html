Mastering grew out of my time in the electroacoustic studio in Leipzig: experimenting with analogue EQs, compressors, and channel strips, listening, and helping friends finish their records. As part of the founding team of the techdub label Ornithopter Records, I was responsible for mastering and looked after the whole catalogue; releases by bands and underground artists from Leipzig and Berlin followed.
